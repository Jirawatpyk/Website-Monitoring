An open incident keeps its escalation ladder, the L3 FYI and the client-update track. Mute never counts as an acknowledgement — the mute sheet says so: “Mute stops new alerts only — INC-0142 keeps escalating until someone acknowledges.”
1
Site is DOWN right now? Mute, don’t schedule A maintenance window can’t start now (or overlap now) on a site that is DOWN or has an open incident. The sheet blocks it and offers Mute instead:
EXAMPLE · MAINTENANCE SHEET, START NOW A window started now won’t hide this incident — it stays open and keeps counting. Mute insteadOpens the mute sheet for MuvMi
2
Write the reason Every mute and maintenance sheet needs a reason before you can submit. It’s kept in the Audit log with the change.
3
End early, edit or cancel from Overview The “Maintenance & mutes” card on Overview lists what’s active and scheduled, with End now and Edit / cancel. Each asks you to confirm and is logged.
