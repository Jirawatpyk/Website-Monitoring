Spec note — weekly summary email (§36) • Figures first. Four tiles replace the old figures line: uptime of all sites, sites with no downtime, DOWN incidents, PROBLEM incidents. Every tile is a count of the week (caption “this week”) and all four are neutral — no red or amber number at the top, so “3” never reads as 3 sites down now. 4 across on desktop, 2 × 2 on a phone. The 30-day line sits under the tiles in plain text: another period, so not a tile. • Badges carry the word. Open: DOWN = solid red, PROBLEM = solid amber (darkest amber text tone as fill, so it holds in light and dark). Recovered: “Was down” / “Was problem” = outline in the same hue. Violet only for Maintenance. • Two lines per incident. Line 1 (15 px): site · started · length (down minutes). Line 2 (13 px, secondary): cause · who acted · root cause or “no root cause yet”. Open incidents first, then newest first. On a phone the badge sits above the lines and line 2 wraps in full — an email can’t expand, so nothing is cut. • Uptime: sites below their weekly target get an amber row each (figure · target · “43 min down · 10 min allowed”); the rest fold into one “N on target” line. Empty sections are left out (Week › Quiet week). • Build: tables + inline styles, system fonts, no images needed to read it; colours from the email tokens matched to AURA; plain-text part kept. Record the new layout as a decision next to D-056.
