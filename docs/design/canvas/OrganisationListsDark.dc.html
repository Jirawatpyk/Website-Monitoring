Spec note — holidays and shared forbidden words (§41, D-069) • Both sit on Settings › Organisation below Clients, above Data kept — one page, an “On this page” line under the tabs. Admin only; view only on phones. • Holidays: a list per year (date · name · kind · rename · remove), “Add holiday” at the section head. One change at a time in a dialog, no note (the date and name say what changed; who and when go to the audit log). Removing a weekday says what that day turns into. • Forbidden words: groups collapsed with a count; Show opens one group. Add and remove one phrase at a time in a dialog with a required note — each phrase is checked against every live site before it can be saved, which a list-and-save bar can’t do per phrase. • Phrase rules: not a single short word (one word under 6 letters, or under 4 Thai characters — “slot” also matches “time slot”); not already in the list; not on any healthy site’s last clean fetch.
